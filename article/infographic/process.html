scripts

Three bars, one claim: "a mass-market robotic pet succeeds by the end of 2026"

Strict
100,000 units sold in 2026
Standard
mass-market retail, strong sales reported
Lenient
widely covered as a hit

Lenient is lenient about the claim's scope, never about the evidence. Social chatter alone meets no bar.

Evidence tiers

  • T1primary: filings, releases, official statistics, price data
  • T2reputable reporting
  • T3a resolved Polymarket or Kalshi market
  • T4the author's own later words: an admission is strong, a victory lap is weak
  • T5social chatter: never sufficient alone

What a cell can say

✓ metP(met) ≥ 0.70 and the evidence has standing
✗ not metP(contradicted) ≥ 0.70
○ unresolvedsilent, thin, conflicting, or the year is not over

The headline number is the generosity gap: lenient hit rate minus strict.
