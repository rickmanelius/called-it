# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-27)

First public release, published with the article "Called It!".

- Branding: "Called It!" with the tagline "Did they actually call it?" and the subtitle "Using AI
  Classifiers to Score a Person's Annual Predictions". Design B, the dark scoreboard, from one token file
  (`scripts/lib/brand.py`): report pages, site, cards, figures, deck, infographic and the explainer
  film all draw from it. Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono are self-hosted.
- Sharing: `share <run>` writes `share.json` and `share.md` (the quote-post text, the author's
  original post to quote, the 1200x630 card, the page link, a one-click X intent URL). Every subject
  page carries Open Graph and Twitter meta tags pointing at its card and a "Share this scorecard"
  section. The article cards and the social previews are the same 1200x630 image; the gallery card is
  the canonical share card.
- The four runs in the repo: Bob Doll, Mike Ippolito, Scott Galloway and Every, each graded at three
  bars with every receipt committed and replayable offline.
