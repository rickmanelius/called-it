# Third subject (finance / business): source discovery

Discovery pass 2026-10-24 (research agent, WebSearch + WebFetch). Not yet graded.

## Recommendation: Bob Doll (Crossmark Global), "10 Predictions for 2026". Runner-up: Scott Galloway.

Doll meets every criterion: exactly ten numbered predictions, free, a 30-plus-year annual ritual, a published final verdict each December that admits misses in his own words, and a mix of magnitudes (GDP 2.0% to 2.5%, a yield band, earnings under +14%, 20 to 25 House seats) and binaries (sector and region outperformance). He also scores himself quarterly during the year, which gives an in-year "author concedes" signal nobody else offers, and a direct comparison between his own grade and the three bars. Galloway is the better narrative match to Every and Ippolito and the bigger name, but his 2026 list is mostly directional ("AI Stocks Correct", "Waymo Dominates") with few hard numbers, and his self-grade is an embedded image rather than text.
